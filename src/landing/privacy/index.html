Privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

This notice explains what personal data khe.ee handles, why, and what your rights are. khe.ee is a personal page. It has no advertising and no user accounts, and it does no profiling or cross-site tracking.

Controller and contact

The controller is Kaido Henrik Elias, who runs khe.ee as a private person. For questions about your data or to use your rights, write to kaidoelias@gmail.com.

Analytics

If you allow analytics, Cloudflare Web Analytics collects aggregate usage and performance metrics such as visits, page views, top paths, referrers, countries, browser and device types, and page load timing. It sets no cookies and is not used for advertising. You can withdraw your consent at any time with the button below; withdrawing does not affect measurement made before then.

Cookies and bot protection

These public pages (the home page, the architecture pages and this notice) set no cookies themselves. Public traffic passes through Cloudflare, whose bot protection, including JavaScript Detections, checks requests to tell people from automated traffic. For this, Cloudflare may set the __cf_bm cookie, which expires after about 30 minutes. Such a security cookie is strictly necessary and needs no consent.

Browser storage

  • app_locale keeps your language choice, so the site opens in the language you picked.
  • khe.analyticsConsent.v1 keeps your analytics choice.
  • Both stay in your browser's localStorage until you clear it. The site does not receive them.

Third-party requests

Fonts and scripts are served from this server. If analytics is allowed, the Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon loads from static.cloudflareinsights.com and sends measurement data to Cloudflare.

Hosting, recipients and transfers

  • The site runs on a private server in Estonia.
  • Public access goes through Cloudflare, Inc. (DNS, tunnel, TLS endpoint, bot protection and, with your consent, analytics). Cloudflare sees request data such as IP address, URL and browser user agent. Cloudflare is based in the United States; the transfer relies on its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • The server's access and error logs record requests, such as the time, requested address, response status and browser details, and may include the IP address. They are kept centrally for 30 days and then deleted.

Trip pages and other services

Pages under khe.ee/r/ are private trip pages, shared only by direct link and hidden from search engines. Their maps may load tiles from an external map service (OpenFreeMap), which then sees your IP address.

Other khe.ee subdomains host private self-hosted applications for the site owner and family. They are not open to public registration, and this notice does not cover them.

Legal bases

  • Analytics: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Hosting, Cloudflare's security features and server logs: legitimate interest in running the site securely and keeping it available (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long data is kept

  • __cf_bm cookie: about 30 minutes.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Browser storage (app_locale, khe.analyticsConsent.v1): until you clear it.
  • Analytics: the site owner sees only aggregate statistics in Cloudflare.

Automated decisions

There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict its processing, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw your analytics consent at any time with the Analytics choices button. To use these rights, write to the contact address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, aki.ee).
